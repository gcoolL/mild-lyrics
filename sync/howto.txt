Demucs is now off by default everywhere, and nothing of mine is running (the one hit above is that pgrep itself; the 2 GB of VRAM is your other apps).

What changed
- dataset, bench, offsets, ttml all read the song as mixed. --stem is the opt-in flag; the old --no-stem is gone.
- train --data now defaults to ~/.cache/mild-lyrics/sync/dataset (mixture clips) instead of the old separated-vocal cache.
- The two stem-trained checkpoints are moved to ~/.cache/mild-lyrics/sync/stem-era/ so nothing can accidentally bench a stem model on mixture audio. sync status correctly reports no model.
- Reason written into dataset.py, bench.py, generate.py and the README: separation is cleaner but invents — smearing and phantom onsets right where a quiet consonant sits under a cymbal — and a timing model taught on those learns to time the artefact. The README's 0.176 s figure is now labelled as the stem-era bar to beat, not a claim about this build.
- Verified: 42/42 tests pass, and I cut 2 songs demucs-free to prove the path (82 clips already in the new dataset).

What to run

python3 -m sync.sync dataset --songs 250      # ~1 min/song, resumable, Ctrl-C safe
python3 -m sync.sync train --steps 20000      # ~1 h on the 4060
python3 -m sync.sync bench --songs 12 --calibrate

Then, to hear it:

python3 -m sync.sync ttml "artist title"      # writes lyrics/Artist - Title.ttml

And the loop, once you have a model worth trusting:

python3 -m sync.sync offsets --songs 40       # measure which copies are displaced
python3 -m sync.sync dataset --songs 250      # re-cut nothing already cut; new songs land at measured offsets
python3 -m sync.sync train --more 8000

Two things to expect. The mixture is a harder input, so heard during training will be lower than the stem-era run — judge it on bench, not on the loss (that run's held-out loss rose from step 6k while alignment kept improving). And sync dataset needs Spotify on port 9222 only for the snapshot, which you already have (233 spl lyrics on disk); the cutting just needs the network for audio.
